# Challenge fit

Read [challenge research](../../../docs/challenges.md), [decisions](../../../docs/decisions.md) and the actual selected brief if available. Paths are relative to this skill directory.

Separate official requirements, published clues and our hypotheses. A title alone cannot establish mandatory technology, a sponsor or prize eligibility. Ask for missing brief details only when they affect the decision; continue comparing supported facts.

For the proposed idea, identify the user, problem, one demo flow and the evidence it can provide for innovation, impact and implementation. Add the partner's actual criteria when known. Assess data/API access, hardware availability, teammate experience and time remaining before the submission deadline.

Recommend a small scope and list the assumptions that could invalidate it. Compare additional challenges only where the same working product meets their criteria. Do not invent a numeric winning probability. Update docs/decisions.md only when the team makes a choice.
