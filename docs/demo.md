## Rehearsal

- Verify the deployed flow on a teammate's computer.
- Reset to a known starting state before each run.
- Check external service access and remaining credits.
- Capture a short recording while the flow works.
- Prepare a labeled fallback recording or sample fixture if connectivity fails; do not describe it as live.
- Remove private credentials and personal information from the recording and slides.

## Two-minute submission video

Suggested structure, not an organizer requirement: problem and user in 20 seconds, working demo in 60 seconds, impact and distinction in 25 seconds, closing in 10 seconds. This totals 1:55. Measure the final encoded file; the guide's maximum is 2:00.

## Stage pitch

Prepare a separate three-minute version and answers for a three-minute question period. Explain what works today, the evidence of impact and what would come next. Rehearse before peer review because the guide says there is no separate practice time afterward.
