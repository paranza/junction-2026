# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Construction engineers inspecting drawings and BIM models on a laptop, with hackathon judges evaluating the same working flow. They need to ask a quantity question, see its source, and correct an interpretation without losing the drawing.

## Product Purpose

Metria answers supported drawing questions with source evidence and calculations. Success is a clear question-and-answer conversation with a large, usable plan preview. Counts, measurements, uncertainty, reviews and inference spending must remain inspectable.

## Brand Personality

Direct, precise, readable. The user selected Antonio’s Metria branding and interface for integration: a neutral workspace, large plan preview, docked question composer and contextual conversation. Existing source inspection and persistent answers remain available.

## Anti-references

Generic AI dashboard decoration, permanent narrow tool panels, tiny text, oversized metadata sections, decorative provenance cards, and a form that pushes its answer below the fold.

## Design Principles

- Lead with the conversation and its actual answer.
- Keep the source drawing visible and stable until the user chooses a detail.
- Reveal advanced analysis and review controls when needed.
- Keep uncertainty and inference spending accessible without dominating the workspace.
- Preserve source coordinates, saved reviews and truthful model results.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No user-specific accommodations were specified. Use readable contrast, keyboard-operable controls, visible focus, labelled inputs, structural mobile layouts and reduced-motion support. Avoid relying on color alone for evidence origin or review state.
